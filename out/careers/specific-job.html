Design Consultant
Design
Fireart is an experience design & technology partner to clients large and small. We believe that great ideas are only as good as how well they perform. Our passion is for pushing the boundaries of our ideas within the limits of what’s possible. We are not satisfied until we are achieving industry-leading results in the market.
Fireart studio is looking for a UX Consultant who will be the coherent chain between the client and design team and will help to improve the design processes.
You are a great fit if you have…
  • at least 3 years of experience building / designing / managing IT projects
  • practical knowledge of product development cycle,
  • previous experience in projects estimation,
  • good understanding of current trends of web/mobile products and solution frameworks,
  • ability to understand customer competition and solution landscape,
  • ability to provide strategic inputs for bid response and controlling the flow and approach,
  • excellent communication and analytical skills
  • excellent command of written and spoken English (C1+).
What will you do:
You will be responsible for evaluating the client needs to provide them with a complete individualized design solution. Your role will also include collaborating with the design team to give the information of client’s preferences, making the estimations of the projects, and improving and suggesting changes to the old design if requires.
